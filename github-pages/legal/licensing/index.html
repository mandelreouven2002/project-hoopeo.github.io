Third-party libraries in detail

Project Hoopeo is built on open-source libraries. Each keeps its own license, and nothing in the licenses of Project Hoopeo (see LICENSING.md) limits the rights those licenses give. This page lists the main libraries of each product, what each is used for, and its license. The complete list of every crate in the binaries, with the full license texts, is in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES, generated with cargo about and shipped with every installer.

The Rust libraries named here are the direct dependencies of each product. DEP Engine, DEP CLI and Hoopeo Studio are distributed as binaries only, and the binaries contain these libraries and their own dependencies, about 590 crate versions across the three products, all under permissive licenses (MIT, Apache 2.0, BSD, ISC, Zlib, CC0, Unicode, CDLA-Permissive 2.0, BSL 1.0), with the exception of a few crates under MPL 2.0, described at the end.

Crates under the Mozilla Public License 2.0

A few crates in the binaries are under MPL 2.0, a license with file-level copyleft: option-ext (through dirs, in all three products), and cssparser, cssparser-macros, dtoa-short, selectors and x509-certificate (through Tauri, in Hoopeo Studio). They are used unmodified, as libraries. The MPL requires that the source of the MPL-covered files be available to recipients; it is, at the repositories named in THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES, and a recipient who wants it may also ask through https://lp.reuven.click. Nothing in the MPL reaches the rest of the binary.
