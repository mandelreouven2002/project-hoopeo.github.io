Names and logos

"Project Hoopeo", "Hoopeo Studio", "DEP Engine", "DEP CLI", "DEP.js", the hoopoe mascot, the Project Hoopeo wordmark and the product icons identify software made by Reuven Mandelovich. The open-source licenses of the code and the documents do not cover them.

You may, without asking:

  • say truthfully that your work implements Database-Enforced Privacy, or is based on, compatible with, or uses DEP Engine, DEP CLI, DEP.js or Hoopeo Studio;
  • use the names in articles, talks, courses and reviews;
  • keep the names and logos in unmodified copies of the software you redistribute.

Please ask first before you:

  • name a product, service, company or domain with "Hoopeo", or with a DEP product name;
  • ship a modified version under the original names or icons (rename a fork, for example "Example Privacy Engine, based on DEP Engine");
  • use the logos in a way that suggests endorsement or partnership.

"Database-Enforced Privacy" and "DEP" describe the concept and may be used freely to refer to it.
