# Privacy notice

This site collects no personal data. This notice says what that means in practice, and what the software of the project does and does not do with data.

## This website

- **No cookies, no analytics, no tracking.** The site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It does not load scripts, fonts, images or any other content from a third party: every file comes from the same site, and the fonts are served from the site itself.
- **What the pages store in your browser.** Two small things, both in your browser's local storage and both only on your own device: the theme you chose with the sun and moon button (`hoopeo-theme`), and, in the playground, the SQL you type in the "Your code" tab (`depsql5.mine`). Neither is sent anywhere. You can remove them by clearing the site's data in your browser.
- **The playground runs entirely in your browser.** The statements you run, and the data of the demo clinic, stay on your device. Nothing is sent to the project or to anyone else.
- **Server logs.** The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may keep server logs, such as the address of the visitor and the page requested, under [GitHub's privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement). The project does not have access to them and does not run its own logs.
- **Links to other sites.** Links to GitHub, to the author's site and to other pages lead to sites with their own policies. The project is not responsible for them.

## The software

DEP Engine, DEP CLI and Hoopeo Studio run on your own machine, or on a server that you run. They send nothing to the project: they have no telemetry, no update check and no license check. The only link to the outside is a Help menu item in Hoopeo Studio that opens lp.reuven.click in your browser when you choose it. The data you put in them stays where you put it.

Hoopeo Studio keeps the connection profiles you save (without passwords) and its local databases on your computer. DEP CLI keeps its command history in a file on your computer.

The software is experimental. See the [terms of use](terms.md).
